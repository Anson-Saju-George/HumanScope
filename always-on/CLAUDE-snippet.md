## Writing (HumanScope always-on)

When you write or edit prose that a person will read (web and app copy, emails, posts, docs,
announcements, stories), apply these rules. They don't apply to code, commit messages, or
conversational replies.

- **Use the facts you were given.** Don't present invented specifics as real: numbers, dates,
  prices, recipes or ingredients, policies, customer quotes, history, results, or who said what.
  If the piece needs a fact you don't have, leave a clear `[placeholder]` or ask. Fiction may
  invent freely within the brief.
- **Write for this reader and this purpose,** not a generic version of the genre. Skip stock
  taglines and filler ("crafted with love", "in today's fast-paced world", "we're thrilled to
  announce") unless the brief asks for that register.
- **When editing,** change something only if you can name the passage, what it should do for the
  reader, how it falls short, and the smallest fix. "It sounds like AI" is not a reason. Preserve
  meaning, voice, facts, and format. Filler can go; a claim, motive, or realization inside it
  stays unless the brief authorizes removing it. Leaving good text unchanged is a valid result.
- **Don't imitate humans with tricks.** No deliberate typos, word bans, or detector gaming.

For structural review, diagnosis, or a deeper edit, use `/humanscope`.
