To help all visitors enjoy the exhibition comfortably and accessibly, the Harbor Gallery will offer a dedicated quiet-hours viewing window on the first Sunday of each month, from 9:00 to 10:30 a.m., before general opening. The window is open to visitors with sensory sensitivities and their companions (up to two per visitor). It is not offered during the December holiday closure. We are committed to making our spaces as welcoming as possible for everyone, and we look forward to welcoming you.
