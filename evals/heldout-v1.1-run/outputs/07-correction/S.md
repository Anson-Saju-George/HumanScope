Remote work may improve productivity, but the evidence so far is limited. In our 2025 survey of 1,200 employees (Internal Report 14), 61% of respondents said they felt more productive at home; the survey was self-reported and did not measure productivity. In a separate comparison, 8 teams that went fully remote shipped features 12% faster on average than 8 teams that stayed hybrid (Internal Report 9), though the sample is small and teams were not randomly assigned, so the difference cannot be attributed to remote work alone.
