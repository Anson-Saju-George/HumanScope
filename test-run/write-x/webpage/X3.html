What we bake

We keep the list short. Everything is made in-house, and when it's gone for the day, it's gone. Come early for the best selection.

Daily

Sourdough

Naturally leavened with our own starter and given a long, slow ferment. Crackly crust, open crumb, a gentle tang.

Daily

Morning buns

Laminated dough rolled up with sugar and spice, baked until the edges caramelize. Best eaten still warm.

Rotating

Seasonal galette

A free-form, flaky-crusted tart filled with whatever's good right now. The filling changes with the seasons, so ask what's on the counter.
