Tomas paid for the groceries in coins, counting them out slowly while the line grew behind him. He had saved them for a month in a jar by the stove. The cashier had been his daughter's friend in school. She waited with her hand open, and he set each coin in it himself, and did not hurry.

When she handed him the receipt she said, "Say hi to Lena for me," and he said he would, though Lena hadn't called since spring.
