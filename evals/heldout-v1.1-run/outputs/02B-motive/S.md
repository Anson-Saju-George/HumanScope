Tomas paid for the groceries in coins, counting them out slowly while the line grew behind him. He had saved the coins for a month in a jar by the stove. The cashier had been his daughter's friend in school, and he set each coin down on the counter where she could see it.

When she handed him the receipt she said, "Say hi to Lena for me," and he said he would, though Lena hadn't called since spring.
