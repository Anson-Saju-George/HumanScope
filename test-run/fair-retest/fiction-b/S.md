I have told this story at three weddings and one funeral, and I have never once told it right. That's the thing about my brother: he doesn't fit in a toast. You start with the time he drove to Albuquerque with a borrowed trumpet and no plan, and people laugh, and then you have to decide whether to tell them about the year after, when he didn't leave his apartment. I always skip that year. I'm telling you now because I think skipping it was a kind of lie, and I'm tired of being the brother who only tells the funny half.

So: the trumpet. He couldn't play it. That was never the point.
