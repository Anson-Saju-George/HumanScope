What we bake

Three things, done well.

Sourdough

Naturally leavened, long fermented, baked dark. The loaf we build the day around.

Morning buns

Laminated dough rolled with sugar and spice, baked until the edges caramelize. Best eaten warm.

Seasonal galette

A free-form tart that changes with whatever's good right now. Ask at the counter what's on today.

Changes with the season

How it works

Walk in. That's it.

We don't take online orders or reservations. Come by during opening hours and pick what you like from the case. When something sells out, it's gone for the day, so earlier is better.
