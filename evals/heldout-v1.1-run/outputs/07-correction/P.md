Our evidence suggests remote work may improve productivity, but it does not yet show it. In our 2025 survey of 1,200 employees (Internal Report 14), 61% of respondents said they felt more productive at home; this is self-reported, and productivity itself was not measured. In a comparison of 8 teams that went fully remote with 8 that stayed hybrid, the remote teams shipped features 12% faster on average (Internal Report 9), though the authors note the sample is small and the teams were not randomly assigned.
